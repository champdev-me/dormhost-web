# Third-party logos

Official brand marks, unmodified. Trademarks belong to their owners and are used
here only to name the runtimes, databases and payment methods the platform
supports.

## Runtimes and databases

| File | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `node.svg`, `python.svg`, `go.svg`, `java.svg`, `ruby.svg`, `php.svg`, `docker.svg`, `mysql.svg`, `mongo.svg`, `postgres.svg` | [devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) `*-original.svg` (MIT) |
| `dotnet.svg` | [dotnet/brand](https://github.com/dotnet/brand) `logo/dotnet-logo.svg` |

All are 1:1 so a square 16x16 chip does not distort them.

Not 1:1: card marks are wordmark-shaped, shown at a fixed height with the width
left to follow. Rendered inside a white badge in the footer since several carry
dark or colour-critical ink that a dark background would wash out.

Replacing any file here means replacing the file, not editing the paths: they
are upstream assets.
