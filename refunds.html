Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 5 August 2026

How billing works

DormHost is prepaid. You add money to your balance, and each day we deduct that day’s cost for the apps you are running. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal, so there is nothing to cancel.

Cancelling

Delete your app in the dashboard, or simply stop topping up. Deductions stop the same day the app stops. You are never charged for a day after you stop.

Refunds on your balance

Unused balance is refundable. Write to support@dormhost.dev from your account email and tell us the amount.

  • We approve or reject within 3 working days and tell you which.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 7 working days.
  • We refund the unused balance. Days already used are not refundable.

If something went wrong

If you were charged for an app that never worked, or charged twice, tell us and we refund it in full — including days used. That is our mistake, not yours.

What is not refundable

  • Days on which your app was running normally.
  • Accounts ended for breaking the acceptable use rules in our terms.

Questions

Email support@dormhost.dev and we will answer within one working day.
