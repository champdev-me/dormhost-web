Privacy policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

Before submitting to PayU: replace [YOUR LEGAL ENTITY NAME] and confirm the payment processor named below matches what you actually use.

What we collect

  • Your email address, because it is how you sign in and how we tell you about your apps.
  • Your apps and their data — the code you deploy and the database it uses.
  • Usage records — which apps ran, for how long, and what they cost, so we can bill correctly and show you your history.
  • Payment records — the amount, date, and a reference from the payment provider.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card or UPI credentials. Those go directly to our payment provider. We receive only a confirmation that a payment succeeded and its reference.

Why we collect it

To run the service, to bill you for it, and to contact you about your account. Nothing else.

Who we share it with

  • Our payment provider, to take payments and issue refunds.
  • Our infrastructure providers, who host the servers your apps run on.
  • Law enforcement, where we are legally required to.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your apps and databases for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after it lapses. Billing records for as long as Indian tax law requires. Email address until you ask us to delete the account.

Your choices

Write to support@dormhost.dev to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account. We act within 30 days.

Security

Traffic to your apps is encrypted. Passwords and access tokens are stored hashed. Each app runs in its own isolated container with its own database credentials.
