Storage and limits

{{limits.storageNote}} The Nap plan includes {{plansByName.nap.storageGb}} GB and the Awake plan {{plansByName.awake.storageGb}} GB, per application.

We keep {{limits.imagesText}}, and nothing older. That is what a rollback uses, and holding only two means the allowance goes to your application rather than to build history you will never return to.

If you go over, we email you rather than deleting anything. Applications that stay over the limit after we have asked can be stopped, and the acceptable use policy covers deliberate attempts to fill a disk.

What is not included

  • There is no free plan. If your site is only HTML, CSS and JavaScript, GitHub Pages will host it free and we would rather you used that.
  • Each plan runs one application. A second application is a second plan.
  • Memory above 512 MB, storage above your plan's allowance and dedicated capacity are not sold on this page. Write to {{company.email}} if you need them.
  • We do not sell domain names. Bring one you already own and we will set up the certificate for it.
  • Uses listed in the acceptable use policy, such as mining, spam, proxies and phishing, are not sold at any price.

Changing or stopping a plan

You can move between plans at any time; the daily rate changes from the day you switch. You can stop at any time with no notice and no fee. See the cancellation policy. If you want money back, the refund policy sets out what we refund and how long it takes.
