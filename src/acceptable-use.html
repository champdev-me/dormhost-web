Acceptable use policy

Last updated {{policies.updated}} · Effective {{policies.effective}}

The short version. ₹{{cheapest.monthly}} a month works because almost every app here is asleep almost all of the time, and because nobody is using us to hurt anyone. Mining, spam and phishing break both of those. They are not allowed, we do look, and accounts that do them are closed without a refund.

This policy is part of the terms and conditions. It applies to everything you run on {{company.tradeName}}: your application, your database, your scheduled jobs and anything your application sends or fetches.

Nothing that steals from people

  • Phishing. Pages that imitate a bank, a college, a payment service, a government portal or any other organisation in order to collect passwords, card numbers, OTPs, Aadhaar or PAN details. This includes pages you describe as a demonstration or a test.
  • Fraud of any kind, including fake shops, fake job postings, fake scholarships, advance-fee scams, investment schemes promising returns, Ponzi and multi-level marketing schemes.
  • Impersonation. Presenting yourself as a person or an organisation you are not, or registering a subdomain intended to be mistaken for someone else's brand.
  • Credential stuffing and account takeover, including hosting the lists or the tooling.

Nothing that mines, or that is really a computer in disguise

  • Cryptocurrency mining of any coin, by any method, including in a visitor's browser, and including "just testing".
  • Hash cracking, brute forcing and password recovery.
  • Distributed compute for reward, including proof-of-work, proof-of-space, bandwidth-sharing networks, airdrop farming, faucet claiming, and any scheme that pays you for consuming a machine's resources.
  • Artificial load. Requests generated to keep your own app awake, to inflate your own traffic figures, or to work around the sleeping behaviour of the Nap plan. If your app must stay running, the Awake plan exists and costs ₹{{plansByName.awake.monthly}}.
  • Deliberate resource abuse, including fork bombs, filling the disk, memory exhaustion, or anything designed to degrade the machine for the other people on it.

Nothing that attacks or hides

  • Attacking other systems, including denial of service, port scanning, vulnerability scanning of hosts you do not own, exploitation attempts, botnet command and control.
  • Malware, meaning writing, hosting, distributing or serving viruses, ransomware, keyloggers, stalkerware or remote access trojans.
  • Open proxies, VPN exit nodes, Tor exit relays and open mail relays. These attract abuse from other people that lands on our IP addresses.
  • Anonymising other people's traffic through your application, whatever it is called.

Nothing that spams

  • Bulk or unsolicited email, SMS or WhatsApp messaging, including through a third-party API from an app hosted here.
  • Mailing list harvesting, and hosting lists you did not collect with consent.
  • Automated posting, following or messaging at scale on other platforms, and bots that break the terms of the service they talk to.
  • Buying bots for tickets, sneakers, exam seats or appointment slots.
  • SEO link farms and doorway pages.

Nothing illegal, and nothing that gets a payment processor closed

  • Child sexual abuse material. This is reported to the authorities immediately and the data is preserved for them, not deleted. There is no warning and no appeal.
  • Content that is illegal in India, including anything prohibited under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it.
  • Pirated software, films, music, books or course material, and torrent trackers or seedboxes for them.
  • Gambling, betting and real-money gaming, which is regulated or prohibited across much of India.
  • Pornographic content.
  • Sale of weapons, drugs, prescription medicines or counterfeit goods.
  • Content inciting violence or hatred against a person or a group.
  • Handling other people's money, including exchanges, custodial wallets, trading bots executing on someone else's funds, or any service that would need an RBI or SEBI registration you do not have.

Nothing that resells us

  • Reselling {{company.tradeName}} capacity as your own hosting service.
  • Sharing one plan between unrelated people or projects. One plan runs one application, for you.
  • Opening several accounts to get around a suspension.

Scraping and automation

Fetching public pages is fine. Ignoring a site's robots.txt, its terms, or its rate limits is not, and neither is scraping personal data about people who have not agreed to it. If a site asks us to stop your scraper, we will pass the request on to you and expect you to comply.

What we actually watch

We monitor the things a host has to monitor to stay online: CPU, memory, disk and network use per container, outbound connection patterns, and abuse reports sent to us by other people. Sustained full CPU on an app with no visitors is the signal that finds mining, and it finds it quickly.

We do not read your source code, your database or your users' data as a matter of routine. We look at them only when we are investigating a specific report or when a court or the law requires it, and we tell you when we have, unless we are legally prevented from doing so.

What happens if you break this policy

What we do depends on what happened.

  • Probably a mistake. A runaway loop, a misconfigured job, a dependency doing something you did not expect. We email you, and usually throttle rather than stop the app. You get a reasonable time to fix it.
  • Clearly deliberate. Mining, phishing, spam, attacks. The application is stopped immediately and the account is suspended. You can write to us and we will look again if you think we got it wrong.
  • Illegal. The account is closed, and we report it and preserve the evidence for the authorities.

Accounts closed for breaking this policy are not refunded. This is the one exception to the refund policy, and it exists because the alternative is honest students paying for other people's mining.

Where an account is suspended rather than closed, your data is kept for {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days so you can get it back. Material that is illegal to hold is an exception and is removed at once.

Telling us about abuse

If something hosted on {{site.appDomain}} is doing any of the above, write to {{company.email}} with the address and what you saw. We read every report and we answer within one working day.
