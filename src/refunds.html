Return & refund policy

Last updated {{policies.updated}} · Effective {{policies.effective}}

The short version. Ask within {{policies.refund.windowDays}} days and you get your money back. It goes to {{policies.refund.mode}}, and reaches you in {{policies.refund.creditDays}} once approved.

There is nothing to return

{{company.tradeName}} sells a digital service. Nothing is shipped to you, so there is nothing to send back and no return address. This policy therefore covers refunds only.

What you can get refunded

Money you have added but not used

Your balance is drawn down daily while your application is on a plan. Whatever is left in your balance is yours, and we will refund it on request at any time. There is no time limit on this and no fee for it.

A monthly plan you just paid for

If you decide within {{policies.refund.windowDays}} days of a payment that the service is not for you, we refund that payment in full, including any days you used. You do not have to give a reason.

A yearly plan

  • Within {{policies.refund.windowDays}} days of buying it: a full refund.
  • After {{policies.refund.windowDays}} days: we work out what you would have paid on the monthly plan for the time you actually used, and refund the difference. On the {{cheapest.name}} plan, using six months of a yearly plan means we keep six months at the monthly rate (₹{{cheapest.monthly}} each) and refund the rest.

An outage that was our fault

If your application was unreachable for more than 24 hours in a row because of a problem at our end, tell us and we will refund those days. We do not count time your application was asleep, time it was down because of a bug in your own code, or time your balance was empty.

A payment that should not have happened

Charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or charged an amount you did not authorise? Tell us and we refund it in full. We do not apply the {{policies.refund.windowDays}} day window to our own mistakes.

What we do not refund

  • Time your application was actually hosted and running, outside the {{policies.refund.windowDays}} day window.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the acceptable use policy. Mining, phishing and spam get you closed and keep your money, and that is deliberate.
  • Payments you have already raised a chargeback on with your bank. Please ask us first. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes months, and asking us takes a day.
  • Domain names, which we do not sell.

How to ask

Email {{company.email}} from the address on your account, with the amount and roughly when you paid. That is all we need. You can also call {{company.phone}} ({{company.phoneHours}}).

How long it takes

  1. We reply within {{policies.refund.decisionDays}} working days to say yes or, rarely, why not.
  2. We send it to {{payments.gateway}} the same day we approve it.
  3. It reaches you in {{policies.refund.creditDays}} from approval. The last part is your bank's timing, not ours, and UPI is usually faster than a card.

How the money reaches you

Refunds go to {{policies.refund.mode}}. We cannot send a refund to a different account, and we will not send it as a bank transfer or a cash payment, because routing it back the way it came is what protects you against fraud.

If the original account is closed, tell us and we will work it out with {{payments.gateway}}. This is slower and usually needs proof that the account was yours.

If you would rather keep the money as balance on your account instead of having it returned to your bank, say so and we will do that instead. It is instant.

Currency

Everything is charged and refunded in Indian Rupees. You get back the same number of rupees you paid. {{company.gst.statement}}
