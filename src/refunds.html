Return & refund policy

Last updated {{policies.updated}} · Effective {{policies.effective}}

The short version. {{policies.refund.short}} A top-up has to be {{policies.refund.minAgeDays}} days old, and what is left has to be worth at least {{policies.refund.minBalanceDays}} days of your plan. Refunds go back to {{policies.refund.mode}}, and reach you {{policies.refund.creditDays}}.

There is nothing to return

{{company.tradeName}} sells a digital service. Nothing is shipped to you, so there is nothing to send back and no return address. This policy therefore covers refunds only.

What you can get refunded

Money you have added but not used

Your balance is drawn down daily while your application is on a plan. Whatever is left is yours, and we refund it on request. You do not have to give a reason, and there is no fee.

Two limits, and they exist because ₹{{cheapest.monthly}} a month is already close to what the machine costs. A top-up can be refunded once it is {{policies.refund.minAgeDays}} days old, so the free trial is not simply bought and returned. And the amount left has to be worth at least {{policies.refund.minBalanceDays}} days of your plan, because a smaller refund costs more in gateway fees than it returns. Anything under that stays on your account as credit, which never expires and which you can spend whenever you like.

A year of credit

Adding a year's price in one payment buys a cheaper daily rate for 365 days, not a year of service. There is no term to break, so refunding it is the same as refunding any other credit: whatever is left in your balance comes back to you on request, under the two limits above. We do not go back and recharge the days you already used at the higher monthly rate.

An outage that was our fault

If your application was unreachable for more than 24 hours in a row because of a problem at our end, tell us and we will refund those days. We do not count time a Nap application was asleep between requests, time it was down because of a bug in your own code, or time it was stopped because your balance was empty.

A payment that should not have happened

Charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or charged an amount you did not authorise? Tell us and we refund it in full. {{policies.refund.faultOfOurs}}

What we do not refund

  • Time your application was actually hosted and running. The price is a daily one, and a day that has been served has been served.
  • A leftover balance worth less than {{policies.refund.minBalanceDays}} days of your plan, or a top-up less than {{policies.refund.minAgeDays}} days old. Both stay on the account as credit rather than going back to your bank.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the acceptable use policy. Mining, phishing and spam get you closed and keep your money, and that is deliberate.
  • Payments you have already raised a chargeback on with your bank. Please ask us first. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes months, and asking us takes a day.
  • Domain names, which we do not sell.

How to ask

Email {{company.email}} from the address on your account, with the amount and roughly when you paid. That is all we need. You can also call {{company.phoneDisplay}} ({{company.phoneHours}}).

How long it takes

  1. We reply within {{policies.refund.decisionDays}} working days to say yes or, rarely, why not.
  2. We send it to {{payments.gateway}} the same day we approve it.
  3. It reaches you in {{policies.refund.creditDays}} from approval. The last part is your bank's timing, not ours, and UPI is usually faster than a card.

How the money reaches you

Refunds go to {{policies.refund.mode}}. We cannot send a refund to a different account, and we will not send it as a bank transfer or a cash payment, because routing it back the way it came is what protects you against fraud.

If the original account is closed, tell us and we will work it out with {{payments.gateway}}. This is slower and usually needs proof that the account was yours.

If you would rather keep the money as balance on your account instead of having it returned to your bank, say so and we will do that instead. It is instant.

Currency

{{payments.currencyNote}} A refund is always made in the currency you were charged in, for the same amount you paid. We do not convert between currencies, so exchange rates cannot move against you between the payment and the refund. {{company.gst.statement}}
