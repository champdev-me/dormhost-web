Why not just use Vercel

The big platforms are built for companies. This one is built for your project.

They are excellent at what they do. What they do is not what a final-year project needs.

A real process, not a function

Vercel and Netlify run your backend as short-lived functions that start and finish around a single request. A Discord bot, a WebSocket, a queue worker, a scraper that runs for two minutes. When you should not use us.

We would rather say this here than take ₹{{cheapest.monthly}} off you for something you can get done better elsewhere.

Your site is just HTML, CSS and JavaScript

Then you do not need us. GitHub Pages hosts static sites free, from the repo you already have, and it does it well. We only charge for things that need a server running behind them: a backend, a database, a login.

You need a global CDN or edge functions

Cloudflare and Vercel have machines in a hundred cities. We have two, in Mumbai and London. If your users are spread across the world and milliseconds matter, use them.

You are running a business on it

We are two servers and one person. That is enough for a college project, a club site or a portfolio. It is not enough for something people's money or jobs depend on. For that, pay a real cloud.

You want to mine crypto or send bulk mail

The whole price depends on most apps being asleep most of the time. Mining, spam, proxies and traffic generators break that, so they are not allowed and we do look. The acceptable use policy is short and says exactly what we mean.

Why it costs ₹{{cheapest.monthly}} and not ₹500.

Most student projects have no traffic

A final-year project gets looked at by your guide, your panel, and whoever you send the link to. The rest of the month it sits there. Other hosts charge you for a machine that is doing nothing.

So we stop charging for nothing

When your app has had no visitors for {{policies.idleMinutes}} minutes, we stop it. It keeps its files and its database. The next request starts it again, measured at about {{policies.wakeMs}} milliseconds, and serves the page.

Questions

Will visitors notice the sleeping?

Almost never. Waking takes around a fifth of a second, which is less than the page would take to load anyway. If it ever runs long you see a short "waking up" screen rather than an error.

Do I need a credit card?

No. In India you can do everything with UPI. Outside India you can use a card, but nothing is stored and nothing renews on its own. You add money when you want to and it is spent a day at a time. Most students top up once a term.

Is there a free plan?

No. A free tier would be paid for by the students on the paid one, and it is the first thing crypto miners sign up for. If your site is static, GitHub Pages is free and better at it than we would be.

What happens if my balance runs out?

Your app sleeps and we email you. Nothing is deleted for {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days, so topping up brings it straight back.

Is my database really included?

Yes, and you choose which one: {{databases.textLong}}. It sits on the same machine as your app, so queries do not cross the internet, and the connection string is in your dashboard.

Which languages can I use?

{{runtimes.detectedText}} are detected and built automatically. For anything else, put a Dockerfile in your repository and we build that, so the language is your choice rather than ours.

Can I deploy a private repo?

Yes. Paste a GitHub personal access token with read access to that repository. We use it to clone your code and nothing else. Revoke it on GitHub and we stop being able to read it, immediately.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within {{policies.refund.windowDays}} days and we refund you to {{policies.refund.mode}}. The refund policy spells out the timings.

What if I need more than 512 MB?

Write to us at {{company.email}}. Bigger plans exist for projects that genuinely need them, but almost no student project does.
