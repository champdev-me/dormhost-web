About us

One person, two servers, and a price that had to work.

{{company.tradeName}} is hosting for college students in India. It exists because putting a final-year project online cost more than the project was worth.

What we do

You point us at a GitHub repository. We work out how to build it, build it, and run it on a server with an address, a certificate and a database attached. That is the whole product. There is no dashboard full of settings you will never touch, and there is nothing to configure before it works.

Why it costs ₹{{cheapest.monthly}}

A student project has almost no traffic. Your guide looks at it, your panel looks at it, and you send the link to a few people. The rest of the month it sits there doing nothing while a server bills you for being switched on.

So we switch it off. After {{policies.idleMinutes}} minutes without a visitor, your application stops. Its files and its database stay exactly where they are. The next request starts it again in about {{policies.wakeMs}} milliseconds, which is faster than the page would have loaded anyway, and serves the page. Because a sleeping app costs us disk and not memory, we can put many more of them on one machine, and that is the entire reason ₹{{cheapest.monthly}} is possible.

We did not invent sleeping. We did put a lot of work into waking up quickly, because a sleeping app that takes half a minute to come back is not really hosting.

Who runs it

{{company.tradeName}} is run by {{company.legalName}}, a software engineer in India, as a {{company.entity}}. It is not a funded startup and there is no team. When you email support, the person who replies is the person who wrote the thing you are emailing about.

We think that is worth telling you rather than hiding. It means support is quick and the answers are real. It also means you should not run a business on us, and we say so on the pricing page too.

Where it runs

On two servers, one in Mumbai and one in London, both Oracle Cloud ARM machines. Your database runs on the same machine as your application, so a query never crosses the internet. Cloudflare sits in front for DNS and protection against attacks.

Everything runs on ordinary Linux, ordinary containers and open source buildpacks. Nothing about your application is locked to us. If you leave, your code is still just a repository that any host can build.

What we will not do

We will not run a free tier. A free tier is paid for by the students on the paid one, and it is the first thing a crypto miner signs up for. If your site is static, GitHub Pages hosts it free and does it better than we would.

We will not sell your data, put trackers on this site, or email you anything that is not about your account. And we will not let anyone use the platform for mining, phishing or spam, because the price only works if the machines are mostly idle and the IP addresses stay clean. The acceptable use policy says exactly where the line is.
