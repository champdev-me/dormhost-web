Privacy policy

Last updated {{policies.updated}} · Effective {{policies.effective}}

The short version. We collect your email, what you deploy, and what we need to bill you. We do not sell any of it, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not read your database unless you ask us to or the law makes us.

This policy explains what {{company.legalName}}, trading as {{company.tradeName}}, does with personal data. It covers this website and the hosting service. It is written to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

Because you gave it to us

  • Your email address, and a password we store only as a hash.
  • Your name, if you give us one.
  • Your GitHub username and, for private repositories, an access token, which we store encrypted.
  • Anything you write to us in an email or a support message.

Because you use the service

  • The repositories you deploy, the builds we run and the logs they produce.
  • Your applications' resource use: CPU, memory, disk, network and how often the app wakes.
  • Server logs containing IP addresses, timestamps and requested paths, kept for 30 days for security and debugging.

Because you paid us

  • Your payment history: amounts, dates and a reference from {{payments.gateway}}.
  • Billing details you enter for an invoice, if you ask for one.

We never see your card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials. Those go straight to {{payments.gateway}}, who are certified to handle them. We get back a reference and whether it worked.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel. This website sets no cookies at all.
  • No Aadhaar, no PAN, no government identity documents.
  • Nothing about your users beyond what lands in ordinary server logs. What your application collects from its own visitors is your responsibility, and you need your own privacy policy for it.

Your code and your database

Your source code, your files and your database are yours. We store and run them because that is the service. We do not read them, mine them, train anything on them or share them.

We will look only when:

  • you ask us to, because you want help with a problem;
  • we are investigating a specific abuse report or a security incident; or
  • a court or a law requires it.

In the first two cases we tell you. In the third we tell you too, unless we are legally prevented from doing so.

Who else sees your data

We use a small number of companies to run the service:

  • {{payments.gateway}}, for payments. They receive your payment details and your email.
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, whose servers in Mumbai and London your application runs on.
  • Cloudflare, which sits in front of the service for DNS and protection against attacks, and sees request metadata.
  • An email provider, for account and support email.

That is the whole list. We do not sell personal data, we do not rent it, and we do not share it for advertising. If we are ever bought or merged, your data would move with the business and we would email you before it did.

Where your data is

Applications run in Mumbai or London, whichever you chose. Account and billing data is held in India. If you pick London, your application and its database are in the United Kingdom, and you should account for that in your own privacy policy if your users are in India.

How we protect it

  • HTTPS everywhere, with certificates issued automatically.
  • Passwords stored as hashes. GitHub tokens stored encrypted.
  • Every application in its own locked-down container, with its own database credentials, unable to reach anyone else's.
  • Administrative access to servers over a private network only, never over the public internet.

No system is perfectly secure. If there is a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and CERT-In within 72 hours of finding out, and tell you what happened and what to do.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • See what we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Correct anything wrong or out of date.
  • Have it erased, except records tax law makes us keep.
  • Withdraw consent, which means closing your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not sort it out.

Email {{company.email}} to use any of these. We answer within {{company.grievanceOfficer.resolveDays}} days, free of charge.

Children

The service is not for children under 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the terms for you. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and we delete it if we find we have.

Email we send you

We send email about your account: deploys, outages, low balance, receipts, policy changes. You cannot opt out of those and still have an account, because they are how the service tells you things. We do not send marketing email. If we ever start, it will be opt-in with an unsubscribe link.

Changes to this policy

If we change something that matters, we will email you at least 30 days before it takes effect, and update the date at the top. Small corrections happen without an email.
