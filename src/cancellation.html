Cancellation policy

Last updated {{policies.updated}} · Effective {{policies.effective}}

The short version. Cancel whenever you like. No notice period, no fee, no phone call to sit through. Billing stops {{policies.cancellation.effect}}, and your data stays put for {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days in case you change your mind.

How to cancel

Either of these works and both are equally final:

  • Delete the application from your dashboard, or close your account there.
  • Email {{company.email}} from the address on your account and say you want to cancel. We action it the same working day and confirm by email.

You do not have to give a reason, and we will not make you talk to anyone before we do it.

When it takes effect

Monthly plans and balance

Cancellation takes effect {{policies.cancellation.effect}}. We stop drawing down your balance from that day. Your application keeps running until the end of the day you have already paid for, then stops.

Whatever is left in your balance can be refunded to you under the refund policy, or left on the account for later. Balance does not expire.

Yearly plans

Cancelling a yearly plan stops it renewing. Your application keeps running to the end of the year you paid for, because you have paid for it.

If you want the money for the remaining months back instead of the remaining months themselves, ask for a refund and we will work it out under the refund policy. Say so when you cancel and we will do both at once.

What happens to your application and your data

  1. Straight away. The application stops. Its address stops resolving. Nothing is deleted.
  2. For {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days. Your code, your files, your database and your backups sit exactly where they were. Restart the plan in that window and everything comes back as it was, at the same address if it is still free.
  3. After {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days. All of it is deleted permanently, including backups. We cannot get it back after that, so please take a copy of your database before then.

Want it gone sooner? Ask, and we will delete everything within 7 days and confirm when it is done.

Cancelling before your application is built

If you pay and then cancel before your first deployment finishes, we refund the full amount. Nothing was delivered, so there is nothing to charge for.

If we cancel

We may end the service too, and here is exactly when:

  • You broke the acceptable use policy. Immediately, without notice, and without a refund. See that policy for what counts.
  • Your balance has been empty for {{policies.dataRetentionDays}} days. We email you first, more than once.
  • We stop offering the service. You get at least {{policies.cancellation.platformNoticeDays}} days' notice by email, help getting your data out, and a refund of everything you have paid for time you will not now get.

Getting your data out

Your database can be exported from your dashboard at any time, and your code is in your own GitHub repository already. If you need help getting something out before a deletion date, email {{company.email}} and we will help. We would rather spend ten minutes on that than have you lose a project.
