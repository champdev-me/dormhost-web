Why it costs ₹99 and not ₹500.

Most student projects have no traffic

A final-year project gets looked at by your guide, your panel, and whoever you send the link to. The rest of the month it sits there. Other hosts charge you for a machine that is doing nothing.

So we stop charging for nothing

When your app has had no visitors for fifteen minutes, we stop it. It keeps its files and its database. The next request starts it again — measured at about 200 milliseconds — and serves the page.

Questions

Will visitors notice the sleeping?

Almost never. Waking takes around a fifth of a second, which is less than the page would take to load anyway. If it ever runs long you see a short "waking up" screen rather than an error.

Do I need a credit card?

No. Add money with UPI whenever you like and it is spent a day at a time. Most students top up once a term.

What happens if my balance runs out?

Your app sleeps and we email you. Nothing is deleted for 30 days, so topping up brings it straight back.

Is my database really included?

Yes — a MongoDB database on the same machine as your app, so queries do not cross the internet. Your connection string is in your dashboard.

Can I deploy a private repo?

Yes. Paste a GitHub personal access token with read access to that repository. We use it to clone your code and nothing else. Revoke it on GitHub and we stop being able to read it, immediately.

Where does my app actually run?

Mumbai by default, London if you choose it. Your database sits on the same machine as your app, so queries between them never touch the internet.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on any paid plan. Point it at us and HTTPS is set up for you.

What if I need more than 512 MB?

Write to us. Bigger plans exist for projects that genuinely need them, but almost no student project does.
